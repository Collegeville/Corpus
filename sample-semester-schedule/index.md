<p>
This is the class meeting and assignment schedule from the Spring 2026 offering of CS373 &mdash;
included as a concrete example of how the Human Skills, AI-Assisted Workflows, and grading
components in this archive actually get sequenced across a semester. Exact dates, topics, and
assignment names shift from one offering to the next; treat the pattern (weekly rhythm,
individual-study days, four Foundations phases building toward a State-of-the-Field talk) as the
durable part.
</p>

## Grading Overview

<p>
The course uses a component-based grading structure: four Foundations assignments building
progressively toward expertise, ongoing dialogue about that expertise, and class participation.
</p>

| Component | Weight | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Topic Expertise | 40% | Periodic dialogue demonstrating "progress in becoming an expert" on your research topic; assessed on growth since course start. |
| Class Participation | 20% | Attendance at all class meetings, "unless prior arrangements are made, or you have an emergency." |
| Foundations Phase A | 10% | Assignment 1 &mdash; a paper and presentation identifying key topic concepts. |
| Foundations Phase B | 10% | Assignment 2 &mdash; a paper explaining key concepts "using entities and relationships via mental models." |
| Foundations Phase C | 10% | Assignment 3 &mdash; an in-depth description, analysis, paper, presentation, and dialogue on one or more technical aspects. |
| State-of-the-Field | 10% | Assignment 4 &mdash; writing and presenting on current field conditions, synthesizing prior work and adding new sections on explorations and future trends. |

## Recurring Commitments

<p>
Two commitments run in parallel with the dated schedule below, rather than landing on a single
day:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Find resources in your topic area</strong> &mdash; ongoing throughout the semester.</li>
  <li><strong>Submit ReVeaL content reflections</strong> &mdash; due by 12 noon CT every Friday, except during breaks.</li>
</ul>
