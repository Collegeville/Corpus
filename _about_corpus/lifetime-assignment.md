The computer science capstone gave students one assignment that was never graded and is never complete: "your most
important work will never be graded or complete." Rather than a deadline-driven deliverable, it's
a set of lifelong practices — habits of integrity and continuous growth meant to outlast the
course, the degree, and any one job.

Everything else in Corpus — every Human Skill, every AI-Assisted Workflow, every Agent Skill — is
in service of these thirteen habits. They're the reason the techniques exist, not a fourteenth
technique alongside them.

## Thirteen Enduring Habits

1. **Keep learning new things** — Maintain curiosity about technology, art, and people through
   reading, listening, building, and questioning.
2. **Own your work and your words** — Take responsibility for all creations and communications
   with honesty, clarity, and kindness.
3. **Seek understanding before certainty** — Replace fear with curiosity when encountering
   unfamiliar concepts; listen deeply and revise beliefs with new evidence.
4. **Write and speak with care** — Treat communication as respect, prioritizing clarity and
   empathy over complexity and ego.
5. **Practice humility and gratitude** — Acknowledge others' contributions to your knowledge;
   express gratitude regularly to remain teachable and connected.
6. **Balance ambition with rest** — Work diligently on meaningful pursuits while recognizing that
   reflection and joy sustain excellence and creativity.
7. **Stay kind in difficult conversations** — Approach disagreement collaboratively, listening
   fully and honoring different perspectives.
8. **Nurture integrity in small decisions** — Choose honesty and fairness in unobserved moments
   because they matter intrinsically.
9. **Pay your learning forward** — Share knowledge freely through teaching and mentoring to
   deepen your own understanding.
10. **Keep revising yourself** — Continually revisit assumptions and refine habits, treating
    personal growth as an ongoing editorial process.
11. **Keep perspective** — Measure life by positive impact on others rather than technological or
    career achievements alone.
12. **Continue showing up** — Demonstrate consistent presence in work, relationships, and
    community with curiosity and care.
13. **Live with wonder** — Find beauty in complexity and joy in problem-solving, appreciating the
    privilege of thinking and creating.
