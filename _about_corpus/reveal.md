---
title: "ReVeaL: Habits of Ongoing Learning"
summary: The habits the course tried to instill for continuing to learn after graduation.
order: 5
---

The computing field doesn't hold still. A professional who stops learning the moment they leave
school falls behind — not dramatically, not all at once, but steadily, in a way that's hard to
notice until it's a real gap. ReVeaL is CS373's name for the habit built to counter that: a
standing weekly practice of self-teaching, kept up for an entire semester until it's no longer a
chore but a reflex.

The mechanics were simple and deliberately unglamorous. Each week, read one article and watch or
listen to one other resource — at least one of the two peer-reviewed, the other from anywhere with
a real connection to computing: a magazine piece, a conference talk, a YouTube explainer. Write a
short reflection on what was actually learned, and submit it before the next class. After the
first few weeks of the semester, those sources had to come from *outside* your own research topic
— the point wasn't to go deeper on what you already knew, but to keep a wide field of view on a
field that changes faster at its edges than at its center.

It's worth noticing what ReVeaL isn't: it isn't graded on brilliance, and it isn't optional once
started. Missing a week couldn't be made up later, because the entire value of the exercise is the
habit itself, not any single week's content — a habit built through consistency, not through
occasionally doing an excellent job of it. AI tools were explicitly allowed to help summarize a
source, on the condition that the student could say how well that summary actually matched their
own understanding — using the tool to save time on the mechanical part, without letting it stand
in for genuinely engaging with the material.

The name is also a convenient expansion of **RVL** — **R**ead, **V**iew, **L**isten: three modes
of learning, and a fitting shorthand for the mix of reading and watching or listening the weekly
practice actually asks for. It's also a small piece of wordplay worth keeping: *ReVeaL* — re-view,
re-learn, re-veal what's changing in the field you've chosen, again and again, for as long as
you're in it.
